# QuickTow coverage

## Towing
Members get one free tow per incident up to 15 miles. Beyond 15 miles, $4 per mile.

## Flat tire
Spare tire installation is covered at no charge. We do not carry replacement tires.

## Dead battery
Jump start is covered. Battery replacement is available for a fee paid to the technician.

## Lockout
One lockout service per year is covered. Replacement keys are not covered.

## Fuel delivery
We deliver up to two gallons of fuel. The member pays for the fuel.

## Accidents
If the vehicle is not drivable after an accident, towing to the nearest repair shop is covered. Police reports are the member's responsibility.
